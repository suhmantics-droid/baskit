Baskit is a free universal wishlist and price tracker made in the UK. Save things you want from any store in one place, sort them into segments with spend caps, and get an honest buy-or-wait score for every item. Paste a product link and Baskit reads the name, price and photo from the store itself. Prices are re-checked for you, cool-off timers take the heat out of impulse buys, and the planning view shows what this month really costs before you spend it. Your basket lives in your browser until you sign in, and then it follows you to any device.
